Questions

Are these questions from the real exam?

No. Every question is original, written for Saydana against a competency in the published blueprint. Using or sharing real exam content breaks SCFHS rules, so we never use it, and we never ask anyone to tell us what was on their exam.

Is Saydana part of SCFHS or Prometric?

No. Saydana is independent. SCFHS offers its own official practice examination, which we recommend as well.

Do I get a certificate or CPD hours?

No. Saydana is a self-study practice bank. It does not issue certificates and does not carry CPD or CME hours.

Who checks the questions?

Each question is checked against its references by an independent reviewer and then approved by a licensed pharmacist before it is published. If you find a mistake, email us and we will correct it.

What happens to my data?

The diagnostic runs entirely in your browser, and your answers are never sent anywhere. If you join the waitlist, we use your email only to tell you about Saydana, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.
